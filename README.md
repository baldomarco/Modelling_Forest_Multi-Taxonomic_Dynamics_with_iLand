# Modelling Forest Multi-Taxonomic Dynamics with iLand
GitHub repository dedicated at the codes of the analysis in the Journal Article: 

Baldo, M., Merganičová, K., Hofmeister, J., Brabec, M., Hošek, J., Dobor, L., Štěpánek, P., Balducci, L., Burrascano, S., &amp; Hlásny, T. (2026). Recovery of multi-taxonomic biodiversity after management cessation in forests of Central Europe. Journal of Applied Ecology.

Data repository: Baldo, M., Merganicova, K., Hofmeister, J., Dobor, L., Brabec, M., Hošek, J., Stepanek, P., Balducci, L., Burrascano, S., & Hlásny, T. (2026). Recovery of multi-taxonomic biodiversity after management cessation in forests of Central Europe. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23220120
